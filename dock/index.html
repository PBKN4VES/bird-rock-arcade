Forklift Frenzy

A truck of green coffee just pulled in. Shelve every pallet in the bay with its coffee's color and code before the clock runs out.

You get 5 seconds to study the warehouse first.

▲▼ drive · ◀▶ turn
Forks ▲▼ lift and lower

Keyboard: arrows or WASD drive and turn.
Forks: E or R up, Q or F down. Honk: Space.

Drive the forks into a pallet at the same height to pick it up. Lower it onto a free shelf to set it down.

Watch for coworkers on the floor: honk so they keep clear. Wrong bay, bumping a coworker or running out of time is a strike. Three strikes ends the shift.
