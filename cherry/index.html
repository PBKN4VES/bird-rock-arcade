Cherry Catch

Coffee cherries fall from the trees. Slide your basket to catch the ripe RED ones.

Green, yellow and orange are not ready, and rocks and bats do not belong in a basket: catch one and you lose a life. A drifting leaf only breaks your combo. Three lives and the harvest is over.

Watch for the bird! When a dotted line shows it has picked a cherry, tap it or press Space to shoo it away. Later on it comes back, so shoo it again. If it steals a cherry, you lose a life.

Catch the shiny items for help: a coffee cup slows things down, a scarecrow keeps the bird away, sunshine ripens every cherry, and a golden cherry gives a life back.
