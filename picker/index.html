Cherry Picker

You have 60 seconds. Tap the cherries when they are RED. Green, yellow and orange ones cost you points, and so do overripe ones.

Higher up, cherries ripen slower and are worth more, but they don't stay ripe long.

A bird will land and walk toward a red cherry. Tap it to shoo it off. Later birds take two taps.
